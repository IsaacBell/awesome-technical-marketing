## SEO

### SEO Toolkits

- [OpenSEO](https://openseo.dev) - AI-powered SEO automation and analysis platform.
- [UberSuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) - All-in-one SEO tool for keyword research, content ideas, and site audits.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) - All-in-one SEO platform for keyword research, audits, and competitor analysis.
- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - SEO toolset for backlink analysis, keyword research, and site audits.
- [Moz](https://moz.com) - SEO software with domain authority and link research.

### Keyword & Content Optimization

- [Clearscope](https://www.clearscope.io) - AI-powered content optimization that aligns articles with what ranks.
- [Surfer](https://surferseo.com) - On-page SEO optimization with content scoring against live SERPs.
- [MarketMuse](https://www.marketmuse.com) - AI content research and optimization for topical authority.
- [Frase](https://www.frase.io) - AI content research and brief generation from search results.
- [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com) - Question-based keyword discovery from autocomplete data.

### Technical SEO

- [Screaming Frog](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) - Website crawler for finding technical SEO issues.
- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) - Official tool for monitoring indexing and search performance.
- [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters) - Indexing and SEO insights for the Bing search engine.
- [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev) - Lab and field data for Core Web Vitals and page speed.
- [Sitebulb](https://www.sitebulb.com) - Desktop crawler with visual technical SEO audits.

### Structured Data & Schema

- [Schema.org](https://schema.org) - Shared vocabulary for structured data markup on the web.
- [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) - Validate structured data eligible for rich results.
- [JSON-LD Playground](https://json-ld.org/playground/) - Test and debug JSON-LD structured data.

## Content & Writing

- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) - Writing assistant for grammar, tone, and clarity.
- [Hemingway App](https://hemingwayapp.com) - Editor that makes writing bold, clear, and concise.
- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) - AI writing assistant for marketing copy and campaigns.
- [Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai) - AI copywriting for ads, emails, and landing pages.

## Link Building & Digital PR

- [BuzzStream](https://www.buzzstream.com) - Outreach workflow and relationship management for link building.
- [HARO](https://www.helpareporter.com) - Connects journalists seeking sources with expert pitches for coverage.
- [Pitchbox](https://pitchbox.com) - Link building and outreach automation platform.
- [NinjaOutreach](https://ninjaoutreach.com) - Influencer and blogger outreach with link tracking.
- [Respona](https://www.respona.com) - Automated link building and digital PR outreach.

## Lead Generation & Prospecting

- [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io) - Email finding and verification service; free tier includes 50 lookups per month.
- [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io) - All-in-one sales intelligence and engagement platform; free tier includes 100 exports per month.
- [Snov.io](https://snov.io) - Email finder, verifier, and cold outreach automation; free tier includes 50 credits per month.
- [Lusha](https://www.lusha.com) - B2B contact data and enrichment platform; free tier includes 40 credits per month.
- [ProxyCurl](https://proxycurl.com) - LinkedIn API for people and company data enrichment; paid tier starts at $10 per month.

## Analytics & Data

### Web Analytics

- [Plausible](https://plausible.io) - Lightweight, privacy-friendly web analytics without cookies.
- [Fathom](https://usefathom.com) - Privacy-first analytics focused on simple, useful metrics.
- [Google Analytics 4](https://analytics.google.com) - Google's event-based analytics platform for websites and apps.
- [Matomo](https://matomo.org) - Open-source web analytics platform you can self-host.
- [Simple Analytics](https://simpleanalytics.com) - Privacy-first analytics with clean dashboards.

### Session Replay & Behavior

- [Hotjar](https://www.hotjar.com) - Heatmaps, session recordings, and behavior analytics.
- [FullStory](https://www.fullstory.com) - Session replay and product experience analytics.
- [Microsoft Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com) - Free heatmaps and session recordings.
- [Mouseflow](https://mouseflow.com) - Session replay, funnels, and form analytics.

### Product Analytics

- [PostHog](https://posthog.com) - Product analytics with session replay, feature flags, and experimentation.
- [Amplitude](https://amplitude.com) - Product analytics for user behavior and retention.
- [Mixpanel](https://mixpanel.com) - Product analytics with funnels, cohorts, and retention.

### Data & Enrichment APIs

- [Abstract API](https://www.abstractapi.com) - Suite of data APIs for email, phone, and IP validation.

## Programmatic SEO

- [AlsoAsked](https://alsoasked.com) - Explore "People Also Ask" question data for content and programmatic research.
- [Programmatic SEO, Explained for Beginners](https://ahrefs.com/blog/programmatic-seo/) - Ahrefs' guide to building keyword-targeted pages at scale from data.
- [Google Sheets](https://www.google.com/sheets/about/) - Spreadsheet-driven content pipelines for low-code programmatic pages.

## AEO & GEO

- [NotFair Plugin](https://github.com/nowork-studio/notfair-plugin) - Open-source Agent Skills for SEO audits, content optimization, and GEO workflows across traditional search and AI answer engines.
- [GEO: Generative Engine Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) - Foundational research paper introducing optimization for AI answer engines.
- [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) - AI answer engine whose sourced responses marketers optimize for.
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) - AI assistant that increasingly shapes how brands approach answer-engine visibility.
- [Microsoft Copilot (Bing)](https://www.bing.com) - AI chat search that summarizes web sources in answers.
